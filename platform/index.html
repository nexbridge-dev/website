One platform for the full stablecoin lifecycle

Issuance

Trust-type JPY stablecoin issuance and redemption, fully API-driven.

  • JPY reserves locked at the trust bank before minting

  • API-First

Custody

HSM + MultiSig key management with policy-enforced approval workflows — built on Thales Luna HSM with custom FM.

  • NexBridge custom FM runs inside the secure boundary
  • M-of-N MultiSig and policy-enforced approvals
  • Thales Luna HSM
  • Custom FM
  • M-of-N MultiSig
